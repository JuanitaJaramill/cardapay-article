# CardaPay: A Cardano Payments Proposal Still at the Conceptual Stage

This repository contains an independent descriptive article about CardaPay, a proposed payment infrastructure built on Cardano.

The article examines:

- What CardaPay proposes to build
- The problem it aims to solve
- Its proposed use of Cardano and Hydra
- Comparable payment initiatives within the Cardano ecosystem
- The scope of its roadmap
- What can and cannot currently be verified from public information

The article is written for a general audience and is based on publicly available and verifiable sources.
